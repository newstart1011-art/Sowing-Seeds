Cultivating Presence, Acting With Intention

Welcome To Sowing Seeds

Today is all about hitting pause on the constant "doing" of your daily routine so you can take a breath and focus on just "being." As a professional, you spend your days pouring your energy into everyone else, completing tasks, and meeting goals. Consider this space a quiet moment to pour some of that energy back into yourself.

While goals are about what you want to achieve, intentions are about how you choose to show up in any given moment. They keep you anchored in the values that matter most to you.

What we will do together:
• Select a plant whose unique qualities resonate with you.
• Create a CLEAR and mindful intention statement that represents how you want to show up.
• Identify potential obstacles to your intention and explore how to overcome them.

2. Creating Your Intention Statement

An intention points to how you want to show up, which naturally guides your actions.

The Goal (Doing)
The Intention (Being)
"Answer client emails."
"I choose to bring patience to communication."
"Finish presentation."
"I am focused and grounded right now."
It should vibe with your unique personality and values.
